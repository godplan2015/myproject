About Us

Kolawole K. Salami (born 2nd Feb 1987) is a Nigerian entrepreneur who is currently the Country Director of Cristabel Resource & Investment Nig.

Cristabel Resource & Investment Nig. is one of the leading logistics companies present in three African countries: Nigeria, Kenya, and Ethiopia. Focused on providing sustainable logistic solutions, Cristabel introduced the "Green 2PL Solutions" product in 2019 to promote eco-friendly logistics.

Our company tailors solutions to each customer's unique needs, emphasizing innovation, technological development, and environmentally conscious processes.
