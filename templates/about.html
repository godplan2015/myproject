About Us

Kolawole K. Salami (born 2nd Feb 1987) is a Nigerian entrepreneur who is currently the Country Director of Cristabel Resource & Investment Nig, since 2019.

Cristabel Resource & Investment Nig is one of the leading logistic companies present in three Africa countries (Nigeria, Kenya, and Ethiopia). It focuses on providing complex logistic solutions with utmost consideration for the environment. In 2019, Cristabel Resource & Investment Nig introduced the “Green 2PL Solutions” logistic product.

Our company prepares tailor-made solutions for each customer and their needs. Extraordinary attention is paid to innovation and the continuous development of technologies and processes.
